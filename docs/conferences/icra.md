# ICRA 2026

Verified: 2026-08-25
Official source: [Paper Presentation Instructions](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/contribute/paper-presentation-instructions/)

## Verified delivery requirements

- Strict 10-minute oral slot.
- 16:9 slides.
- Own laptop over HDMI; test in the assigned room and carry a USB backup.

The official advice is at most 7 minutes speaking and at least 3 minutes for questions. Treat 420 seconds as the talk target and 600 seconds as the hard slot when planning this presentation type.

## Branding evidence

The presenter instructions do not mandate a slide template, font, palette, or logo. ICRA's separate [brand identity material](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/about/brand-identity-and-guidelines/) governs uses of conference branding; it is not evidence that every paper author must brand their deck. Style selection remains independent from these delivery requirements.

## Artifact notes

[Final Author Instructions](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/contribute/final-author-instructions/) separately specify a mandatory graphical abstract, optional A4 PDF slides, and a 7-minute 16:9 MP4 for non-attending authors. Confirm whether those apply to your participation mode; do not treat the online PDF page dimensions as the live-screen aspect ratio.

## Authoring emphasis

- Plan a 6:30-7:00 main path and reserve the rest for questions/changeover.
- Keep optional derivations and ablations lower in vertical stacks.
- Test HDMI scaling, embedded fonts, video codecs, speaker notes, and offline playback on the presenting laptop.
