# ICML 2026

Verified: 2026-08-25
Official sources: [Presenter Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/PresenterInstructions), [Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorInstructions), and [Accessible Papers and Talks](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2023/AccessiblePapersAndTalks)

## Verified delivery requirements

- 15-minute oral slot.
- Plan to speak for 12 minutes, leaving time for questions and speaker changeover.
- Present from your own laptop over full-size HDMI.
- Do not use a physical laser pointer; use a mouse-based pointer visible across screens and remote views.

No mandatory aspect ratio, slide template, palette, conference font, or logo is published on the presenter page.

## Accessibility expectation

ICML's standing guidance expects accessible and inclusive papers and talks. Apply at least:

- clear, simple, uncrowded slides with large sans-serif text and whitespace;
- high contrast;
- color plus shape, position, line style, pattern, or direct label;
- no flashing graphics;
- a brief adjacent description for important visuals;
- verbal communication of important visual content and repetition of audience questions.

A high-contrast render is a useful audit, not a substitute for making figures and narration accessible.

## Authoring emphasis

- Rehearse a 11:30-12:00 main path.
- Use screen-visible highlights/direct labels instead of pointing at projected pixels.
- Upload the recommended PDF deck when appropriate and verify fonts/links in the PDF artifact.
