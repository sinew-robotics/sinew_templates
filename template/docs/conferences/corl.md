# CoRL 2026

Verified: 2026-08-25
Current source: [Instruction for Authors](https://2026.corl.org/contributions/instruction-for-authors)

## Known current information

Every accepted paper receives a poster; selected papers are invited for an oral spotlight.

## Presenter rules pending

At verification time, the current material did not publish oral timing, spotlight-video workflow, slide aspect ratio, palette, font, template, or logo requirements. Keep these items marked pending in the delivery checklist.

For historical context only, [CoRL 2025](https://2025.corl.org/contributions/instruction-for-presentations) used an 8-minute oral plus 2-minute Q&A and a 60-second 1080p MP4 spotlight with a five-second title frame. Those values are not encoded as 2026 requirements.

## Authoring emphasis

- Do not infer a branding or slide-template requirement from the absence of current presenter rules.
- Prepare an assertion-evidence oral deck and a separate poster/spotlight narrative; they serve different attention contexts.
- Re-verify before locking aspect ratio, slide count, recording, or upload automation.
