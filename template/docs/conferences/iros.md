# IROS 2026

Verified: 2026-08-25
Current source reviewed: [remote/travel-difficulty speaker instructions](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/attend/instructions-for-speakers-who-experience-travel-difficulties-and-cannot-attend/)

## Known current information

- Normal expectation is in-person oral and poster presentation; a proxy is preferred when an author cannot travel.
- The published page refers to 3-minute lightning slots and focused presentations with 9 minutes speaking plus 2 minutes for questions.
- Safe remote recording durations are 2:45 and 9:30 respectively.
- Remote recording uses H.264 and places a portrait video of the speaker, roughly one-quarter of the screen, beside the slides.

## Unknown or format-dependent

The reviewed page is not a complete general presenter manual. It does not establish one timing mode for every paper, a general live-slide aspect ratio, mandatory palette/font/logo, or the final upload workflow. Keep timing and export behavior unresolved until the assigned format is confirmed.

Do not copy IROS 2025's five-minute, centrally uploaded workflow into a 2026 talk. Confirm the assigned presentation type and current instructions in the conference app/site.

## Authoring emphasis

- Build the long version first, then a separate lightning narrative if assigned; do not merely accelerate the same dense deck.
- Keep one unmistakable contribution, one method view, and one result in the short path.
- If remote, design a safe area that remains readable beside a quarter-screen portrait window.
